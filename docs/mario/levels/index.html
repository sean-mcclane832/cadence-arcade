MARIO, LEVEL BY LEVEL

One Cadence brain (cadence-net 0.91.1) plays the levels in order, learning the whole time. Updated 2026-10-29 14:08.

First sight

How far it gets on a level it has never trained on: median x of 3 solo attempts (bar), best of the 3 (white line). 1-1 is marked "pre": the brain started from last night's 1-1 training.

What's happening

  • One Cadence brain plays Super Mario Bros. in order, 1-1, 1-2, 1-3, 1-4, 2-1 ..., the way a person works through the game. It keeps learning the whole time; it is never reset between levels.
  • First sight is the test that matters: 3 solo attempts on a level before any practice on it. If it is learning to play Mario rather than memorizing levels, first-sight distance should rise as it goes.
  • Practice on a level: it plays on its own and learns from the reward (going right, not dying), copies its own good stretches, and gets a few coached tries where a look-ahead planner writes down what it would have pressed. The planner never takes the controls.
  • About a tenth of its play is an earlier level again, so old levels are revisited but not drilled. It moves on after clearing a level solo (flag in 2 of 3) or after about 38 minutes of practice.
  • Started from last night's 1-1 brain (round 10, the best saved checkpoint; round 8's was never saved), so 1-1 is not a real first sight.
